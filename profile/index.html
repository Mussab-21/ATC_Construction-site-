Built on Precision.

Azaan Trading & Contracting Company (ATC) is a premier engineering and construction firm dedicated to delivering world-class infrastructure. Established with a vision to redefine standards, we operate across Saudi Arabia and Pakistan, executing complex projects with uncompromising quality.

From high-security defense infrastructure to cutting-edge civil developments, ATC acts as a trusted partner for governments and multinational corporations. Our approach blends technical discipline, rapid execution, and a strict adherence to international safety protocols.
