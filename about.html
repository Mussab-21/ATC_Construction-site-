Our Story

Engineering Capability. Proven Track Record.

Azaan Trading & Contracting (ATC) was established with a clear mandate: to deliver complex engineering projects with the technical rigour and quality discipline that demanding clients — from military authorities to industrial operators — require.

Operating across Pakistan and Saudi Arabia, ATC brings together nine specialist divisions under one management structure, ensuring seamless coordination from initial design through final commissioning. Our clients include Army Welfare Trust, Herfy Foods, Crown Gulf, ROSHAN, NEOM, and US Military Facilities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Every engagement is treated as a long-term relationship. ATC's commercial work is matched by an equally strong operations and maintenance capability — ensuring client assets continue to perform after handover.
